# Case Studies

Three high-risk districts were selected from the latest district acquisition risk output.
Each memo is grounded in transaction risk, merchant saturation, and replacement-candidate logic.

## 1. 서초구

- Verdict: `Very High` (78.1)
- Core objections: pricing looks stretched relative to district peers; recent transaction pricing is unstable
- Latest month: `202603` with 21 trades and a median `sqm` price of 2633.1 (10k KRW)
- Six-month price change: +112.9%
- Six-month transaction change: -53.3%
- Saturation signal: 2163.7 stores per admin-dong, food-store share 17.4%
- Sample reliability: `High`
- Dominant categories: 변호사:2673 | 경영 컨설팅업:2650 | 부동산 중개/대리업:2052
- Why the model flagged it: 가격 부담 점수 98.6점으로 상위권입니다 / 상권 과밀 점수 71.2점으로 경쟁 압박이 큽니다 / 최근 가격 변동성이 커서 체결 레벨 재확인이 필요합니다
- Lower-risk substitutes: 금천구 | 관악구 | 동대문구
- Why substitutes look better: lower overall acquisition risk; lighter merchant saturation | lower overall acquisition risk; stronger transaction liquidity; lighter merchant saturation
- Field checks before underwriting: verify whether recent asking prices still clear at transaction levels; review large outlier trades and separate whole-building deals from strata units
